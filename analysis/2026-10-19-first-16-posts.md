## TikTok findings
1. **Two tiers, nothing broke out.** Every post landed either at ~750-900 views (passed TikTok's first test batch, then
   stopped) or at ~80-320 (failed the first batch). No post went past ~900, so TikTok never pushed one to a second, bigger
   audience. Moving past the first batch needs watch-through + shares + saves; we have 5 shares in 16 posts.
2. **F1 is our TikTok category.** All three F1 posts that reached the batch have 11-13% like rates - 2-8x football and
   cars. F1 viewers like; football viewers watch and scroll.
3. **Football: a name gets the views, only a moment gets the likes.** Messi top 5 (a compilation, mixed sources): 810
   views, 1.5%. Mbappe: 3.4%. Yamal with a claim ("19. Already past Messi."): 8.5%. Ronaldo "still him": low reach
   (243) - nothing in the first second said why to watch.
4. **Cars: spectacle or story pass, beauty films fail.** Passed: 911 Dakar (story "they built it again") and Tesla
   (flying car). Failed at ~260: Taycan x2, Bugatti x2, Tourbillon - pretty product films with no event.
5. **Saves = the best signal we have.** "The greatest show on earth" has 12 saves + 3 shares, more than any other post.
   It is the lyric-led edit with the question caption ("best moment of 2026 so far?").
6. **Length didn't matter.** Singapore at 59 s and Greatest Show at 16 s ended in the same place.
7. **Antonelli = the platform difference.** 79 views on TikTok (Oct 14, the account's second day, almost no
   followers, the TikTok caption used #dexedits instead of a stake) vs 9,736 on YouTube, where people were already
   searching his name that week. YouTube Shorts rewards a searched name; TikTok rewards the first second + shares.

## What changes
- F1 every day stays the anchor; it is what TikTok likes. Give it a stake + question caption (the Greatest Show shape).
- Car slot: an event, a record, a first, a "would you" - never only "beautiful car".
- Football: one player, one moment, one claim in the first line. No compilations unless every clip is official and sharp.
- Every caption ends in a question someone can answer or send to a friend (shares/saves are what we lack).
- Same name that people search this week on YouTube (Antonelli lesson): the title says the name + the stake.
