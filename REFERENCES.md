# Reference edits – what we learned

Every reference video Desmond sends is studied (cuts vs drum hits, effects, text, story, sound) and the lessons
land here and in `EDITING_RULES.md`. The videos and audio themselves are NOT stored in this public repo
(copyright) – they live in the session's working folder, which is temporary. This file is the permanent memory.

Goal: learn the techniques, never copy an edit.

## Style ideas worth reusing
- **Car-edit style on other subjects ("it should be nature").** Use car-edit techniques on a different subject –
  e.g. plants and nature: garage-configurator UI where a flower's colour / petals swap on every beat; same-spot
  match cuts across many flowers; time-lapse bloom as the "engine start"; macro details as "detail shots".
  The whole edit is about the new subject; only the technique comes from car edits.
- **Video-game garage / configurator** (ref B2): arrows ‹ ›, colour swatches, wheel picker drawn over real
  footage; paint / wheels / interior change on each beat. "Need For Speed vibes".
- **Same-spot match cuts** (ref B3): many subjects, all framed identically (front, centred), one per beat –
  the eye stays still, the subject swaps underneath.
- **Logos / objects as letters** (ref B4): brand logos arranged to spell words ("LOVE … PEOPLE INSIDE");
  subjects cut out onto black like stickers; creator logo sting at the end.
- **Series evolution** (ref B1): one character redrawn per era, the era's logo above it, one era per beat.
- **Aura edit** (ref A1): long calm slow-mo intro, then cut on every snare with white flash, photocopy frames,
  colour/BW strobe, radial blur. If the sound has SPEECH, the picture must show what is said (the "Formula Max"
  sound says "handshake between the two teammates… Formula One, Formula Max" – show exactly that).
- **Cinematic lyric edit** (ref A2): 2.4:1 letterbox, a cut every ~2 s on the downbeat, lyrics as small
  word-by-word subtitles with key words big and red, every shot matching its lyric.
