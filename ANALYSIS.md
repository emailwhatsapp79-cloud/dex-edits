## What to do (learned)
1. **Strong stories beat everything.** The two best TikToks are a real controversy (Marc/Marco contact, McLaren DSQ). Pick the
   moment people argue about; a stake in the first 2 s (see EDITING_RULES "STORY FIRST").
2. **MotoGP is as good as F1** (YouTube avg ~1,670 vs ~1,470 for F1 in this window; small sample). Keep the multi-series plan:
   F1 + MotoGP lead. **Supercars / DTM did worse** (~900) and NASCAR got 0 - do not make more one-off series edits unless there is a
   big headline (title decided, controversy).
3. **Check every post 1 h after it goes live.** Two YouTube Shorts got ~0 views (Leclerc WrOoQ-kkFa4 = 3, NASCAR DWENgFgsyms = 0)
   and the 2:14 Max story got 3. Open them in YouTube Studio for a processing / visibility problem; re-upload if stuck.
4. **Length (replaced by the Studio data above + EDITING_RULES "NOV 17": 15-20 s, 25 s max).** Old note: 22-34 s Shorts are the sweet spot on YouTube; the 24 s Max hype beat the 134 s Max story (1,547 vs 3). Longer cuts only on
   TikTok (the 57 s MotoGP edit worked there). Never post >60 s to YouTube.
5. **Call to action:** use "an F1 edit every day" ONLY on F1 posts. Non-F1 posts (DTM, Supercars, MotoGP...) must say
   "a race edit every day". DTM and Supercars captions wrongly said F1 on both platforms.
6. **Slots:** no reliable winner. Nov 11-13 morning (07:00 UTC) led; Nov 14-17 evening (17:00 UTC) led (Race 400 2,128, Max hype
   1,547, Vegas 2025 TikTok 1,566). Keep 3 posts a day, put the strongest edit of the day at 18:00 Lagos.
7. **Comments** are near zero on most posts since the poll comments were stopped (Nov 14, Desmond's call). Only controversial
   posts draw them (MotoGP: 11 on YouTube, 7 on TikTok). Reply to every real comment (CLAUDE.md "REPLY TO COMMENTS").
8. **Vegas weekend:** make fresh edits the same day from the official highlights (quali Sat 21 ~05:00 Lagos, race Sun 22 ~05:00).
   Fresh footage is what moved the numbers in the Nov 5-8 spike.
